## 🔧 Architectural Decisions Ratified

Recorded in `.claudedocs/evolution/reservation-capability.md` (an Evolution Log entry — deliberately
**not** an ADR yet; this is one real proven case, not a pattern confirmed twice):

1. **The pipeline is canonical**: `Validate → Resolve Resource → Working Hours → Conflict Check →
   Create Reservation → Post Actions`. Every current and future Reservation Strategy goes through
   these six stages, in this order.
2. **Resource's definition is settled**: a bookable thing with its own calendar — not a business
   entity itself. `Resource ≠ Doctor` conceptually, even though v1 shares one table pragmatically.
3. **Reschedule is registered as its own deferred capability** — not Update Status, not Cancel, not
   Create. Needs its own Working Hours re-check and its own Conflict Check re-run.
4. **Standing rule: no `ReservationStrategy` registry, `ReservationProfile`, or ADR extraction
   until Barber (the second real case) succeeds.**
