Conclusion: 6fps was a real, measurable under-sampling for this footage's actual motion
(confirmed quantitatively, not assumed) -- 12fps is the evidence-backed density, not a re-guess.
Motion is also NOT uniform across the clip (ranges from mean=1.39 at the calm entrance to
mean=21.03 at the busy closing shot) -- a uniform frame rate is a simplification; an adaptive/
variable-density extraction (denser during high-motion segments) would be more efficient still,
but wasn't built this pass -- flagged as a real open question, not assumed necessary.
